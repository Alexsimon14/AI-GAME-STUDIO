# First Market Research

Use the Market Researcher role and the `market-research` skill.

Research the current Android mobile-game market to identify opportunities for Game 001, built by a very small studio in Godot.

Prioritize:
- relatively simple development;
- 1–5 minute sessions or similarly approachable play;
- progression and replayability;
- future cosmetic skins;
- optional rewarded ads;
- possible future season pass only if it fits naturally;
- low infrastructure cost;
- no required real-time multiplayer for the MVP.

Analyze current competitors and recurring player complaints. Do not develop a game and do not choose the final concept. Produce an evidence-based shortlist of opportunity spaces and save it as `reports/market/YYYY-MM-first-game-opportunities.md`.
